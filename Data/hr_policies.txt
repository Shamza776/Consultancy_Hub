This section sets forth the employment policies, benefits framework, and workplace
conduct standards applicable to all Company personnel.

3.0 LEAVE AND TIME-OFF POLICY

Clause 3.1: Annual Leave
1. Entitlement: Full-time Employees accrue twenty-one (21) working days of paid
   annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.75 days/month.
2. Approval: Annual leave requests must be submitted via the HR Portal at least
   five (5) business days in advance and approved by the Employee's direct
   supervisor.
3. Carryover: A maximum of five (5) unused annual leave days may be carried
   over into the following calendar year. Any excess is forfeited unless local
   labor law requires otherwise.

Clause 3.2: Sick Leave
1. Entitlement: Employees are entitled to fourteen (14) days of fully paid sick
   leave per calendar year.
2. Documentation: A medical certificate is required for any sick leave exceeding
   two (2) consecutive working days.
3. Extended Illness: Sick leave beyond fourteen (14) days is subject to the
   Company's Extended Medical Leave policy, requiring HR case management.

Clause 3.3: Parental Leave
1. Maternity Leave: Ninety (90) calendar days of paid maternity leave, in
   accordance with applicable local labor law.
2. Paternity Leave: Fourteen (14) calendar days of paid paternity leave, to be
   taken within three (3) months of the child's birth.

4.0 ONBOARDING PROCEDURES

Clause 4.1: Pre-Boarding Checklist
1. HR issues an offer letter and employment contract via the Digital Signature
   Platform.
2. New hires must complete background verification and reference checks prior
   to their start date.
3. IT provisioning (laptop, email, system access) must be requested by HR at
   least three (3) business days before the employee's start date.

Clause 4.2: First-Week Requirements
1. New Employees must complete mandatory Compliance and Code of Conduct
   training within their first five (5) working days.
2. A designated onboarding buddy will be assigned to every new hire for their
   first thirty (30) days.

5.0 GRIEVANCE AND DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES

Clause 5.1: Filing a Grievance
1. Employees may raise a workplace grievance informally with their manager, or
   formally in writing to HR via the Grievance Reporting Form.
2. HR must acknowledge receipt of a formal grievance within two (2) business
   days and complete an investigation within fifteen (15) business days.

Clause 5.2: Disciplinary Process
1. The Company follows a progressive discipline model: Verbal Warning, Written
   Warning, Final Written Warning, and Termination, except in cases of gross
   misconduct which may result in immediate termination.
2. Employees are entitled to representation during any formal disciplinary
   hearing.

6.0 BENEFITS OVERVIEW

Clause 6.1: Health Insurance
1. The Company provides comprehensive medical, dental, and optical coverage
   for the Employee and up to four (4) registered dependents, effective from
   the first day of employment.

Clause 6.2: Remote Work Policy
1. Employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to three (3) days per week,
   subject to manager approval and role suitability.
2. Remote work does not alter core working hours or availability expectations.
