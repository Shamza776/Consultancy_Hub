CUSTOMER SUCCESS OPERATIONS PLAYBOOK
Company Confidential — Internal Use Only

This section defines customer support standards, escalation procedures, and
retention protocols for the Customer Success Department.

11.0 SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLAs)

Clause 11.1: Response Time Standards
1. Enterprise-tier customers: initial response within one (1) hour, 24/7.
2. Professional-tier customers: initial response within four (4) business
   hours.
3. Standard-tier customers: initial response within one (1) business day.

Clause 11.2: Resolution Targets
1. Critical issues (service outage, data loss risk): resolution target within
   eight (8) hours.
2. Major issues (significant feature failure): resolution target within three
   (3) business days.
3. Minor issues (cosmetic bugs, feature requests): resolution target within
   ten (10) business days or added to product roadmap review.

12.0 ESCALATION PROCEDURES

Clause 12.1: Tier 1 to Tier 2 Escalation
1. Front-line Customer Success Representatives must escalate to Tier 2
   Technical Support if an issue is unresolved after thirty (30) minutes of
   troubleshooting or requires engineering-level investigation.

Clause 12.2: Executive Escalation
1. Any complaint involving contract cancellation threats, legal action, or
   public/media exposure risk must be immediately escalated to the Customer
   Success Director and copied to the Legal Department.
2. Executive escalations require an acknowledgment response to the customer
   within two (2) hours.

13.0 REFUND AND CREDIT POLICY

Clause 13.1: Service Credits
1. Customers experiencing SLA breaches are entitled to service credits per the
   signed Master Services Agreement, typically 5% of monthly fees per hour of
   qualifying downtime, capped at 100% of the monthly invoice.

Clause 13.2: Refund Approval Thresholds
1. Refunds up to $500 may be approved directly by a Customer Success Manager.
2. Refunds between $500 and $5,000 require Director approval.
3. Refunds exceeding $5,000 require both Director and Finance approval.

14.0 CHURN PREVENTION PROTOCOL

Clause 14.1: At-Risk Account Identification
1. Accounts flagged with declining product usage (>40% drop month-over-month),
   unresolved critical tickets, or non-renewal signals must be logged in the
   Customer Health Dashboard as "At-Risk."

Clause 14.2: Retention Playbook
1. At-Risk accounts must be assigned a Retention Specialist within two (2)
   business days.
2. A structured save plan (usage review call, success plan revision, or
   pricing review) must be proposed to the customer within one (1) week of
   flagging.

15.0 CUSTOMER DATA HANDLING

Clause 15.1: Confidentiality
1. Customer Success personnel must never share one customer's account details,
   pricing, or usage data with another customer under any circumstances.
