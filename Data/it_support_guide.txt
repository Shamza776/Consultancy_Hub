INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY SUPPORT & SECURITY HANDBOOK
Company Confidential — Internal Use Only

This section outlines IT support procedures, acceptable use standards, and
security protocols applicable to all Company personnel and systems.

7.0 ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

Clause 7.1: Company Equipment
1. Company-issued laptops and devices are for business use. Limited personal
   use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties or violate
   Company policy.
2. Installation of unauthorized or unlicensed software on Company devices is
   strictly prohibited.

Clause 7.2: Password and Account Security
1. All Company accounts require multi-factor authentication (MFA) where
   supported.
2. Passwords must be a minimum of twelve (12) characters and rotated every
   ninety (90) days.
3. Sharing account credentials with any other individual, including colleagues,
   is a violation of this policy.

Clause 7.3: Remote Access
1. Remote access to internal systems must be conducted exclusively through the
   Company-approved VPN client.
2. Use of public Wi-Fi to access Company systems without VPN protection is
   prohibited.

8.0 IT SUPPORT PROCEDURES

Clause 8.1: Submitting a Support Ticket
1. All IT issues must be logged through the IT Service Desk Portal, categorized
   by priority: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
2. Critical issues (full system outage, security breach) must additionally be
   reported by phone to the IT Emergency Line.

Clause 8.2: Response Time Service Levels
1. Critical priority tickets: response within thirty (30) minutes, resolution
   target within four (4) hours.
2. High priority tickets: response within two (2) hours, resolution target
   within one (1) business day.
3. Medium/Low priority tickets: response within one (1) business day,
   resolution target within three (3) business days.

Clause 8.3: Hardware Requests
1. New hardware requests (laptops, monitors, peripherals) must be submitted via
   the Asset Request Form and approved by the requestor's Department Head.
2. Standard hardware requests are fulfilled within five (5) business days;
   expedited requests require Director-level approval.

9.0 SECURITY INCIDENT REPORTING

Clause 9.1: Suspected Breach Protocol
1. Any Employee who suspects a security incident (phishing email, malware,
   unauthorized access, lost/stolen device) must report it immediately to the
   IT Security Team via the Incident Hotline or security@company.internal.
2. Employees must not attempt to independently investigate or remediate a
   suspected breach.

Clause 9.2: Device Loss or Theft
1. Loss or theft of a Company device must be reported within one (1) hour of
   discovery to enable remote wipe procedures.
2. A police report is required for insurance and asset recovery purposes.

10.0 DATA HANDLING

Clause 10.1: Confidential Data Storage
1. Confidential or sensitive Company data must only be stored on approved
   Company systems (SharePoint, Company cloud drive), never on personal
   devices or unauthorized third-party cloud services.
